Privacy

Your privacy

What you pray here stays on your device.

What this site keeps, and where

So you can pick up where you left off, each novena remembers:

  • the days you mark as prayed, and your start date
  • the intention you type
  • your text size and light or dark choice, and whether you chose “Not now” when offered to add the site to your home screen

These are saved in your browser’s storage on this device only. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts and no sign-in. On a shared phone or computer, someone using the same browser could see them.

What this site does not do

No ads, no cookies, and no analytics or tracking scripts running in your browser. The fonts and pictures come from this site itself, not from Google or other services.

The Share button sends only the novena’s link, its title and a short line. It never includes your intention or your progress.

Offline copy

So the novenas open without signal, your browser keeps a copy of this site’s pages, pictures and fonts. That copy holds nothing personal.

Visits and hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify’s servers log each page request so they can deliver the site and keep it secure. A request carries details such as the time, the page asked for, the site that linked you here, your browser type and your IP address.

From these logs we see visits only as simple totals, such as how many pages were requested and whether any failed. We don’t use them to find out who you are. Your intentions and progress are never in them, because they never leave your device. Netlify keeps its logs for a limited time under its own privacy policy. Links to other websites follow those sites’ own policies.

Clear what is saved here

This deletes your progress, start dates, intentions and settings for every novena on this device. It cannot be undone.

You can also clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings.
