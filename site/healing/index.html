      1. 1Opening Prayer

        Pause for a moment of silence before God, then make the Sign of the Cross. Where you see N., say the name of the person you are praying for.

        In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

        Come, Holy Spirit, fill the hearts of Your faithful and kindle in them the fire of Your love.

        Heavenly Father, You know the illness that N. is carrying: the pain and weariness of body and spirit, and the worry of all who love them. We bring this suffering to You, the Lord who heals, and we trust in Your loving care more than in our fear. Bless all who tend the sick. Today we ask St. Raphael, messenger of Your healing; St. Camillus de Lellis, servant of the sick; and St. Bernadette, patient in suffering, to join their prayers with ours before Your throne, through Christ our Lord. Amen.

      4. 4Closing Prayer

        Our Lady of Lourdes, pray for us.

        St. Raphael, pray for us.

        St. Camillus de Lellis, pray for us.

        St. Bernadette, pray for us.

        Lord Jesus, Divine Physician, lay Your healing hand on N. and on all the sick. Guide those who care for them, give us strength and peace, and let Your holy will be done. Amen.

        Memorare

        Remember, O most gracious Virgin Mary, that never was it known that anyone who fled to your protection, implored your help, or sought your intercession was left unaided. Inspired by this confidence, I fly unto you, O Virgin of virgins, my Mother. To you do I come; before you I stand, sinful and sorrowful. O Mother of the Word Incarnate, despise not my petitions, but in your mercy hear and answer me. Amen.

      5. 5Pray together

        Our Father

        Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come, thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread, and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

        Hail Mary

        Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

        Glory Be

        Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit, as it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.

        Today’s step

      How to Pray This Novena

      1. Pause for a moment of silence before God.
      2. Pray the Opening Prayer, beginning with the Sign of the Cross.
      3. Read the day’s Scripture and reflection, then pray the day’s prayer, naming your intention.
      4. Pray the Closing Prayer and the Memorare.
      5. End with an Our Father, a Hail Mary and a Glory Be, and the Sign of the Cross. Then take today’s step with you.

      Praying with others

      One person leads each part; all respond pray for us and Amen, and say the closing prayers together. The prayers say “we”; if you pray alone, say “I”.

      Alongside prayer

      If you can, go to Confession and attend Mass during these 9 days. Ask a priest about the Anointing of the Sick, which is for anyone seriously ill or facing major surgery, not only the dying. Pair prayer with good care: follow medical advice, offer practical help (Day 6), and be wary of anyone who promises a sure cure. At “N.”, say the sick person’s name; if you are praying for yourself, say “me”.

      The 3 Intercessors

      St. Raphael

      Archangel · Feast · Sep 29 (with Sts. Michael & Gabriel)

      Patron of healing & the sick

      Archangel of the Book of Tobit, one of the seven holy angels who present the prayers of the saints. His name means “God heals”; he guided young Tobias and brought healing to Tobit’s eyes.

      St. Camillus de Lellis

      1550–1614 · Feast · Jul 14

      Patron of the sick, nurses & hospitals

      Soldier and gambler, converted in 1575, who gave his life to the sick. Though a leg wound troubled him for the rest of his life, he founded the Camillians, the Ministers of the Sick.

      St. Bernadette

      1844–1879 · Feast · Apr 16

      Patron of the sick

      Miller’s daughter to whom Our Lady appeared at Lourdes in 1858. As a Sister of Charity at Nevers she bore long illness with patience, and died at 35.

      “Beloved, I pray that all may go well with you and that you may be in health; I know that it is well with your soul.”
      3 John 2
