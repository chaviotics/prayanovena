      1. 1Opening Prayer

        Pause for a moment of silence before God, then make the Sign of the Cross. Where you see N., say the name of the person you are praying for.

        In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

        Come, Holy Spirit, fill the hearts of Your faithful and kindle in them the fire of Your love.

        Heavenly Father, Giver of every life, You know N. and the child they are waiting for: their joy, their hopes and their worries. We place this pregnancy in Your hands. Keep mother and child safe, give this little one health and strength, and, whatever lies ahead, prepare N. to welcome the gift You are entrusting to them. Today we ask Our Lady of the Visitation; St. Gerard Majella, friend of mothers and their children; St. Elizabeth, who waited long and rejoiced; and St. Joseph, guardian of Mary and her Child, to join their prayers with ours, through Christ our Lord. Amen.

      4. 4Closing Prayer

        Our Lady of the Visitation, pray for us.

        St. Gerard Majella, pray for us.

        St. Elizabeth, pray for us.

        St. Joseph, pray for us.

        Lord Jesus, once carried beneath Your mother’s heart, bless N. and the child they are waiting for. Keep them safe in Your love, give them peace in every season of waiting, and let Your holy will be done. Amen.

        Memorare

        Remember, O most gracious Virgin Mary, that never was it known that anyone who fled to your protection, implored your help, or sought your intercession was left unaided. Inspired by this confidence, I fly unto you, O Virgin of virgins, my Mother. To you do I come; before you I stand, sinful and sorrowful. O Mother of the Word Incarnate, despise not my petitions, but in your mercy hear and answer me. Amen.

        Today’s step

      How to Pray This Novena

      1. Pause for a moment of silence before God.
      2. Pray the Opening Prayer, beginning with the Sign of the Cross.
      3. Read the day’s Scripture and reflection, then pray the day’s prayer, naming your intention.
      4. Pray the Closing Prayer and the Memorare.
      5. End with an Our Father, a Hail Mary and a Glory Be, and the Sign of the Cross. Then take today’s step with you.

      Praying with others

      One person leads each part; all respond pray for us and Amen, and say the closing prayers together. The prayers say “we”; if you pray alone, say “I”.

      Alongside prayer

      If you can, go to Confession and attend Mass during these 9 days, and ask a priest or deacon for the Church’s blessing of parents before childbirth. Pair prayer with good care: checkups, rest and help from others (Day 3). A medal is a sign of prayer, not a charm. At “N.”, say the parents’ names, or “me” or “us” if you pray for yourself.

      If the road is hard

      If you face a hard diagnosis, a high-risk pregnancy or a loss, you are not alone, and it is never a sign that you prayed too little (Day 8). Lean on your doctor and your parish. If a newborn is in danger of death, ask for Baptism at once; in an emergency, anyone may baptize.

      The 3 Intercessors

      St. Gerard Majella

      1726–1755 · Feast · Oct 16

      Patron of expectant mothers & childbirth

      Tailor’s son from southern Italy, turned away by the Capuchins for poor health, who became a Redemptorist lay brother. Remembered for his charity and wonders, he died at 29.

      St. Elizabeth

      1st century · Feast · Nov 5

      Patron of expectant mothers

      Wife of the priest Zechariah; for many years the couple had no child. When Mary visited her, the baby in her womb leaped for joy, and she gave birth to John the Baptist.

      St. Joseph

      1st century · Feasts · Mar 19 & May 1

      Patron of fathers & families

      Husband of Mary and foster father of Jesus, who took Mary into his home, brought her safely to Bethlehem and protected the Child and His mother on the flight into Egypt.

      “The Lord bless you and keep you: The Lord make his face to shine upon you, and be gracious to you: The Lord lift up his countenance upon you, and give you peace.”
      Numbers 6:24–26
