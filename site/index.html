How a novena works

Learn more

  • 9 days, 1 intention

    A novena is 9 days of prayer for a single intention, after the Apostles and Mary, who prayed together before Pentecost (Acts 1:14).

  • About 10 minutes a day

    Each day has an opening prayer, a Scripture passage and reflection, the day’s prayer, and the closing prayers.

  • A devotion, not the Mass

    A novena helps us grow in faith, come to know God better and bring Him our intentions. It leads us to the Holy Mass, the Church’s highest worship, and never takes its place. Why

Common questions

Can I pray more than 1 novena at a time?

Yes. You can pray 2 novenas side by side, say one for healing and one for finances, and each keeps its own progress here. Keep to what you can pray with attention. Or pray one novena and bring both intentions to it.

What if I miss a day?

Simply pick up where you left off. A novena is a devotion, not a test.

Where is my intention saved?

Only on your phone or computer. Your progress, start date and intention are never uploaded, and there are no accounts. Privacy

Are we praying to the saints instead of God?

No. We worship God alone. When we pray to a saint, we are asking that saint to pray for us, the way we ask a friend, because the saints are alive with God. Why we ask the saints

Does a novena guarantee an answer?

No novena comes with a guarantee. We bring our intention to God and trust His answer, which may be yes, not yet, or a grace we did not expect.
