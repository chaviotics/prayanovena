      1. 1Opening Prayer

        Pause for a moment of silence before God, then make the Sign of the Cross.

        In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

        Come, Holy Spirit, fill the hearts of Your faithful and kindle in them the fire of Your love.

        Heavenly Father, You know every financial burden we carry and every debt that weighs on our minds. We place our trust in Your loving providence rather than in our own fear. Today we ask St. Joseph, faithful guardian and provider; St. Rita of Cascia, advocate of the impossible; and St. Jude Thaddeus, patron of desperate cases, to join their prayers with ours before Your throne, through Christ our Lord. Amen.

        Mary, Help of Christians, pray for us.

        Lord Jesus, guide our steps, bless our honest work, grant us wisdom in stewardship, and lead us to freedom from debt according to Your holy will. Amen.

        Memorare to St. Joseph

        Remember, O most chaste spouse of the Virgin Mary, that never was it known that anyone who implored your help and sought your intercession was left unassisted. Full of confidence in your power, I fly unto you and beg your protection. Despise not, O Guardian of the Redeemer, my humble supplication, but in your bounty, hear and answer me. Amen.

      How to Pray This Novena

      1. Pause for a moment of silence before God.
      2. Pray the Opening Prayer, beginning with the Sign of the Cross.
      3. Read the day’s Scripture and reflection, then pray the day’s prayer, naming your intention.
      4. Pray the Closing Prayer and the Memorare to St. Joseph.
      5. End with an Our Father, a Hail Mary and a Glory Be, and the Sign of the Cross. Then take today’s step with you.

      Praying with others

      One person leads each part; all respond pray for us and Amen, and say the closing prayers together. The prayers say “we”; if you pray alone, say “I”.

      Alongside prayer

      If you can, go to Confession and attend Mass during these 9 days, and pair prayer with prudent action: an honest budget, a talk with creditors, wise counsel (Day 4).

      The 3 Intercessors

      St. Joseph

      1st century · Feasts · Mar 19 & May 1

      Patron of workers & families

      Carpenter of Nazareth and foster-father of Jesus, who provided for the Holy Family by honest labor. Invoked for steady work and trust in God’s provision.

      St. Rita of Cascia

      1381–1457 · Feast · May 22

      Patron of impossible causes

      Wife, mother, widow and Augustinian nun who bore long family trials with patience and forgiveness, and became a peacemaker in her town.

      St. Jude Thaddeus

      1st century · Feast · Oct 28 (with St. Simon)

      Patron of desperate cases

      Apostle and martyr, traditionally linked to the Letter of Jude. Long invoked by the faithful when all seems hopeless.
