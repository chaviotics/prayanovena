## Saint facts

Source short names: FM = franciscanmedia.org saint of the day; Brit = britannica.com; NA = New Advent Catholic Encyclopedia (Francis, cathen/06221a); Celano = Thomas of Celano, First Life (Fordham Medieval Sourcebook, stfran-lives.asp); Athanasius = Life of Antony (via EWTN library, st-anthony-abbot-patriarch-of-monks-5192); VN = Vatican News saint page, Jan 17; EWTN-news = EWTN News articles on St Anthony of the Desert and on St Martin de Porres; EWTN-litany = Litany of Loreto text.

### St. Francis of Assisi (c. 1181–1226, feast Oct 4)
- Born 1181/82, died 3 Oct 1226 (FM, Brit, NA). Patron of ecology, proclaimed by John Paul II in 1979 (FM, Brit); patron of animals is traditional (FM).
- Day 2: preached to a flock of birds near Bevagna, urging them to praise their Creator, and blamed himself for not doing so before (Celano; NA). Early biography.
- Day 3: freed a hare caught in a trap, which ran to his lap; returned a fish he was given to the water (Celano). Early biography.
- Day 8: Canticle of the Creatures praises God for "Sister Death"; he repeated that verse on his deathbed in 1226 (FM, NA).
- The Wolf of Gubbio (Little Flowers, legend) is not used.

### St. Anthony the Abbot (c. 251–356, feast Jan 17)
- Dates and feast (FM, VN, EWTN-news).
- Day 4: at about twenty, moved by the Gospel of the rich young man, gave away his inheritance; lived as a hermit in the desert, weaving mats and tending a small garden (Athanasius, Life of Antony, ch. 2 and later chapters; FM; EWTN library). Early biography by one who knew him.
- Day 7 and bio: traditional protector of domestic animals and livestock (EWTN-news, "co-patron of animals"; not an official decree); animals blessed on his feast, including in Rome (VN; EWTN-news); often shown with a pig and a bell (VN, FM).
- Legends (healed piglets, lions at Paul the Hermit's grave) are not used.

### St. Martin de Porres (1579–1639, feast Nov 3)
- Born 9 Dec 1579 in Lima, died 3 Nov 1639; canonized 1962 (FM, Brit). Patron of social justice and racial harmony (Brit, FM).
- Day 5: apprenticed at twelve to a barber-surgeon, learning to treat wounds and prepare medicines; as a Dominican brother nursed the sick in the priory infirmary (FM, Brit).
- Day 5: "I cure them, but God heals them": single secondary source (EWTN-news), so introduced as "He is remembered as saying".
- Day 6: kept stray cats and dogs at his sister's house and excused the kitchen mice because they were hungry (FM; Brit on his compassion for strays and kitchen vermin). Introduced as "it is remembered that".
- Not used: the "veterinary hospital" claim and the dog-cat-mouse dish story (not sourced).

### Our Lady, Health of the Sick
- "Health of the Sick" (Salus infirmorum) is an invocation of the Litany of Loreto (EWTN-litany). No date or apparition claimed.

## Church teaching (paraphrased, not quoted)
- CCC 2416 (Day 1, tips): animals are God's creatures in His providential care; we owe them kindness.
- CCC 2418 (Day 9, tips): love for animals must not take the affection due only to persons, nor money owed first to relieve human misery.
- No claim is made about animals in heaven; the Church has no definitive teaching.

## Review changes (2026-11-25, independent review)
