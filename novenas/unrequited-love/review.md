# Review: Novena for When Love Is Not Returned (draft)

Independent Catholic content review, 2026-11-22 (file as it stood at 08:20, after St. Raphael was replaced by St. Pier Giorgio Frassati and the Marian title became Our Lady of Sorrows). Verdict: doctrinally sound, gentle and well paced; all lengths within limits; capitals and digits/words correct; Day 9 step matches the skill; each intercessor leads 3 days (Frassati 1, 4, 8; Joseph 3, 6, 7; Our Lady 2, 5, 9).

**Confirmed:** free-will framing (no prayer asks God to change the other person's heart; opening prayer says so daily); vocation presented fairly; no promised outcome; CCC 1604 quoted accurately; the closing prayer's Sacred Heart line is sound (John 1:11; echoes the devotion to St. Margaret Mary without quoting her); Frassati facts (1901–1925, Turin, wealthy family, St. Vincent de Paul from 1918, drawn to a young woman and renounced her, unsure his parents would approve, confided in his sister Luciana, thousands at his funeral, "Verso l'alto", canonized 7 Sep 2025 with Carlo Acutis, feast Jul 4, "patron of young people" as popular usage); Joseph (Matthew 1:19 paraphrase, no recorded words, feasts); Our Lady of Sorrows (Luke 2:35 paraphrased, John 19:25); Jeremiah 29:11 acceptable as long as nothing frames it as finding a spouse; helpline tip and the "love spell" line (gayuma) are well placed; Day 4 step heads off obsessive pursuit.

## Findings and what was done

1. **MUST, applied.** Frassati's love was never turned down: he gave it up and never told her. "Who knew this sorrow" (opening prayer) became "who knew the pain of letting love go"; Day 1 prayer "who knew the sorrow of a love given up".
2. **SHOULD, applied.** Day 1 "more" rewritten to the exact account: "knew a similar ache", strained parents' marriage, never told her (source: FrassatiUSA, "His love for Laura", citing the canonization documents).
3. **SHOULD, applied.** Day 5 "more": Cana no longer adds "young couple", "before anyone else" or "quietly".
4. **SHOULD, applied.** Day 9: "we are not where we began" → "No prayer of these nine days was wasted, and God has heard every one."
5. **SHOULD, applied.** Day 6: "after every other love on earth has ended" → "will never stop loving us"; "one person said no" → "one person does not love us back"; "one person’s no" → "one unreturned love".
6. **SHOULD, applied.** "Alongside prayer" tip now covers a beloved who is married or in a relationship, a spouse whose love has cooled (priest or marriage counselor), and friends praying for someone else.
7. **SHOULD, applied.** `images.md` brought up to date.
8. **NIT, applied.** Day 4 "more" paraphrases his well-attested letter to Isidoro Bonini (in giving her up he wished for her happiness); the tram-fare line was already gone.
9. **NIT, applied.** Day 3: "forgive ourselves for hoping" → "make peace with ourselves for having hoped".
10. **NIT, applied.** Day 4 prayer: "a pure gift" → "a free gift".
11. **NIT, applied.** Kicker "9 days of healing and freedom to love".
12. **NIT, applied.** Helpline tip names sleeping, eating or working.
13. **NIT, partly applied.** Day 8 "religious life" → "consecrated life"; "finding a spouse" dropped from keywords. Kept the Sassoferrato Virgin in Prayer for the Marian face (no Mater Dolorosa image available offline) and Day 5's "whether or not that includes N.".

Sources the reviewer used: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pier_Giorgio_Frassati; frassatiusa-org.sites.ecatholic.com/his-love-for-laura; churchpop.com (the woman Frassati gave up for his family); vatican.va CCC 1604.
