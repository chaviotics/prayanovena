## Saints and teaching

- **St. Martha:** Luke 10:38–42; John 11:5, 11:20–27; John 12:2. Feast July 29 (since 2021 the memorial of Sts. Martha, Mary and Lazarus). Patronage of servants, cooks and hospitality is traditional.
- **St. Cecilia:** Roman virgin martyr, named in the Roman Canon (Eucharistic Prayer I). Patronage of musicians comes from the antiphon of her feast, from the account of her martyrdom: "Cantantibus organis, Caecilia in corde suo soli Domino decantabat" (while the instruments played, Cecilia sang in her heart to the Lord alone). Dates are traditional (3rd century). Feast November 22.
- **St. Thérèse of Lisieux:** 1873–1897; entered the Carmel of Lisieux at 15, died at 24; Story of a Soul, Manuscript A: she says she should be desolate for having slept during her hours of prayer and her thanksgivings after Communion, but is not, since little children please their parents as much asleep as awake (paraphrased in Day 6 `more`); wrote "pious recreations" (plays) for her community and acted in them (e.g. as Joan of Arc, 1895); co-patron of missions (Pius XI, 1927); Doctor of the Church (1997). "Everything is grace" (Tout est grâce) is from her last conversations (1897). Her love of her own littleness is paraphrased (Letter 197), not quoted. Feast October 1.
- **CCC 2008:** God freely chose to associate us with the work of His grace (paraphrased in Day 2).
- **Mary, Handmaid of the Lord:** Luke 1:38.
