# Catholic review: Novena for Serving in Ministry

Independent review (fresh reviewer, no stake in the draft), 2026-11-24. Doctrine, saint facts, sacraments and tone found sound; nothing promises an outcome.

Applied:
- Day 3 `more`: Mary "stood with the servants" → "spoke to the servants" (the text says only that she spoke to them).
- Day 3 `refl`: "He works the wonder" → "He does what only He can do" (no hint of a promised result).
- Day 8 `more`: "If you don't, pray for…" → "If you don't know who is coming, pray for…".
- Day 9 `more`: Thérèse "summed up her life in three words" → "said simply" (Last Conversations, 5 June 1897, said about dying without the sacraments).
- St. Martha `patron`: "servers" → "servants" (traditional patronage; "servers" reads as altar servers).
- St. Thérèse `bio`: "her sisters" → "her community" (blood vs. religious sisters).

Not applied:
- Martha's feast line "(with Sts. Mary & Lazarus)": kept short for the card; noted in sources.md.
- Day 9 step "thank God for them together": the skill fixes this step's wording for every novena.
