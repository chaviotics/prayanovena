# Novena for Safe Travels: review

Independent Catholic content review (fresh subagent, no stake in the draft), 2026-11-25. It found doctrine, titles, Scripture quotes inside the reflections, Cabrini's facts and feasts, CCC 336 and the DMW tip sound, and the Psalm 121 handling on Day 9 careful.

Applied:
- Day 7 paragraph 2: Anna *had* been told an angel would go with Tobias (Tobit 5:20–21), so "She did not know…" became "Tobit had told her that a good angel would go with him, yet she could not stop watching the road." Redundant "Each day… every day" fixed.
- ogDescription: "St. Cabrini" → "St. Frances Cabrini".
- Day 9: gratitude no longer assumes every journey went well ("for His company on every mile"; the prayer commends "all whose journeys ended in sorrow").
- Day 8 prayer: "Grant eternal rest to those who died far from home."
- Day 3: "God gives each of us a guardian angel"; "we are not left alone" (was "never sent out alone").
- Day 2 paragraph 2: "far from all he knew" in place of a speculative "different language and customs".
- Working abroad tip: "Never let anyone keep your passport".

Not applied: an extra "even when the road is hard" line in the opening prayer (Day 1 and Day 9 already say it); embassy/OWWA hotline numbers (print space; the tip already points to the embassy or consulate).
