# Independent Catholic and picture review (2026-11-24)

A fresh Opus reviewer with no stake in the draft checked doctrine, saint facts (against the cited sources and vatican.va for CCC 2731/1128), guarantees, pastoral care, titles and all 18 day images. Verdict: sound; 4 must-fix items, all applied:

1. Day 2 title "The Desert Is Not a Punishment" was too absolute (Israel's forty years were a punishment, Num 14:33–34; CCC 2731 allows dryness to call for conversion) → "Led Into the Desert".
2. Alongside-prayer tip "it is not a sign that you have failed" contradicted the line before it → "it is not a sign that God has left you, and He can use it to draw you closer".
3. Day 7 Elsheimer crop nearly black → re-cropped to take in the moon and its reflection; alt rewritten to describe the crop.
4. Day 2 alt: Hagar holds a jar, not a water skin.

Suggestions applied: Day 1 "Jesus walked it first" made concrete (Gethsemane and the cross); Day 4 CCC 1128 paraphrase narrowed (holiness or feelings of those who receive); Day 4 "no one else has the words"; Day 3 Romans 8:26 sentence cut (length); tip: "a prayer time you can manage, kept faithfully" instead of "short"; doctor tip ties 2 weeks to the symptoms; Day 9 prayer adds "or only when we see You face to face"; Chantal bio says 41 years (matching Day 2); picture credits: Melone at National Gallery London, Siemiradzki at State Russian Museum, Domenichino at Versailles, Van Gogh June 1888, Tissot passage John 10, Fra Angelico alt/look describe the Communion; Domenichino whole picture trimmed above the harp's bare-breasted gilded caryatid; Chantal portrait licence reworded to PD-old-anon.
