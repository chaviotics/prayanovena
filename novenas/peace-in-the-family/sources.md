# Novena for Peace in the Family: sources

## Scripture (RSV-CE)

All texts are RSV-CE, checked word for word on 2026-11-25 with `tools/check_verses.py` against the RSVCE chapter files in the GitHub repository `andrewheekin/obsidian-holy-bible`.

Expected differences only: in Numbers 6:24–26 the source writes small-caps LORD as "theLord" (we print "LORD"). In Matthew 18:22 Jesus' speech runs on into the parable of verse 23, so the source has no closing quotation mark at the end of verse 22; we close the quotation where our excerpt ends. No passage uses thee/thou; "O woman" (John 2:4) is a vocative, not archaic address.

## Church teaching

- CCC 2304: peace is “the tranquillity of order,” the work of justice and the effect of charity (Day 2).
- CCC 2383: separation of spouses, with the bond maintained, can be legitimate in cases provided for by canon law (tip "If home is not safe").
- Embolism before Communion in the Roman Missal ("Lord Jesus Christ, who said to your Apostles: Peace I leave you, my peace I give you… graciously grant her peace and unity") (Day 2).

## Our Lady, Queen of Families

St. John Paul II added the invocation "Queen of families" to the Litany of Loreto in 1995 (Day 7, litany).

## St. Joseph

- Name, dates, feasts, alt text copied from `novenas/relief-from-debt`.
- Patron of the Universal Church: Pius IX, decree *Quemadmodum Deus*, 1870 (bio).
- Litany of St. Joseph (USCCB, usccb.org, "Litany of Saint Joseph"): "Joseph most just", "Glory of family life", "Cornerstone of families", "Head of the Holy Family" (Day 1 quotes "Glory of family life" and "Cornerstone of families").
- Pope Francis, *Patris Corde* (2020), section headings "A beloved father", "A tender and loving father" (background only).
- "Patron of families" is a popular, traditional patronage, not a formal decree.

## St. Monica

All from Augustine's *Confessions*, New Advent translation (J. G. Pilkington):

- Book 9, ch. 9 (newadvent.org/fathers/110109.htm): Patricius "as he was earnest in friendship, so was he violent in anger" (quoted, Day 3); she did not resist him while angry, and once he was calm "would give him a reason for her conduct"; she won over a hostile mother-in-law by patience and meekness, after which they "lived together with a wonderful sweetness of mutual good-will"; between quarreling people "she would disclose nothing about the one unto the other, save what might avail to their reconcilement"; Patricius came to faith near the end of his life (not used; we do not claim his baptism).
- Book 3, ch. 12 (newadvent.org/fathers/110103.htm): an unnamed bishop told her, "it is not possible that the son of these tears should perish" (quoted, Day 6; the bishop is not named).
- Book 8, ch. 12 (newadvent.org/fathers/110108.htm): at Augustine's conversion "she leaps for joy, and triumphs, and blesses You" (quoted, Day 6).
- Name, dates, feast, alt text copied from `novenas/habitual-sin`.

## St. Rita of Cascia

Certain: died 22 May 1457 at Cascia; canonized 1900 (Catholic Encyclopedia, "St. Rita of Cascia"; Wikipedia). Everything else is presented as "tradition says", following the life by Agostino Cavallucci (1610) as summarized in the Catholic Encyclopedia and Wikipedia: married about 18 years to Paolo Mancini, a man of hard temper; two sons; Paolo was murdered; she publicly forgave the killers and prayed her sons would not take vengeance; she worked to reconcile the feuding families before entering the Augustinian monastery at Cascia.

Deliberately omitted as legendary or disputed: the bees, the dry vine, levitation into the monastery, and the story that she asked God to take her sons rather than let them commit murder.

"Patron of difficult marriages" is a popular, traditional patronage.

Name, dates, feast, alt text copied from `novenas/impossible-cases`.

## Choices

- Memorare: Marian, following `safe-travels` (St. Joseph is one of several intercessors and the novena's Marian title, Queen of Families, leads the litany). The St. Joseph Memorare is used in `relief-from-debt`, where Joseph is the lead intercessor.
- Saints by day: Joseph Day 1; Monica Days 3 and 6; Rita Days 5 and 8; Mary, Queen of Families, Days 2, 4 and 7; all on Day 9.
- Safety: tip "If home is not safe" (no hotline numbers); Day 3 frames Monica's patience as waiting for anger to cool, not submission; Day 8 names limits, distance and safety.
