## Church teaching cited

- CCC 1830 (the gifts are permanent dispositions that make us docile to the Spirit's promptings) and CCC 1831 (the seven gifts: wisdom, understanding, counsel, fortitude, knowledge, piety, fear of the Lord). These are the gifts, not the fruits (Galatians 5:22–23; CCC 1832), and the novena only speaks of gifts.
- CCC 159 (faith and reason, faith and science cannot truly conflict).
- CCC 1303 (Confirmation "increases the gifts of the Holy Spirit in us"), in the "Alongside prayer" tip.
- Pope St. John Paul II, *Fides et Ratio* (1998): its opening image of faith and reason as two wings on which the human spirit rises to the truth. Paraphrased, not quoted.

## Saint facts used

- **St. Augustine (354–430), feast Aug 28.** Son of St. Monica; teacher of rhetoric; years with the Manichees before his conversion; the garden in Milan and "Take up and read" (*tolle, lege*), Romans 13:13–14 (Confessions VIII); baptized in 387; Bishop of Hippo; Confessions I.1, paraphrased in indirect speech ("He has made us for Himself, and our heart is restless until it rests in Him") rather than quoted from a particular translation; Letter 118 to the young student Dioscorus: the way to truth is "first humility, second humility, third humility" (paraphrased). Patron of theologians (traditional).
- **St. Albert the Great (c. 1200–1280), feast Nov 15.** Dominican friar; Bishop of Regensburg; teacher of St. Thomas Aquinas; wrote on plants, animals and minerals; canonized and declared a Doctor of the Church in 1931 (Pius XI); named patron of natural scientists by Pius XII in 1941.
- **St. Hildegard of Bingen (1098–1179), feast Sep 17.** Benedictine abbess, mystic and visionary; visions from childhood; sought counsel (from St. Bernard of Clairvaux; not named in the text) and submitted her writings to the Church; Pope Eugenius III encouraged her to keep writing (Synod of Trier, 1147–48; not named in the text); wrote on plants, trees, stones and animals (*Physica*) and on the body and medicine; composed sacred music; equivalent canonization and Doctor of the Church in 2012 (Benedict XVI). The patron line reads "Mystic & Doctor of the Church" because she has no single well-established patronage.
- **Our Lady of Good Counsel.** No historical claims are made beyond the title.

## Refocus on the gift of knowledge (2026-11-20)
Chav's friend asks above all for knowledge, so the novena was renamed "Novena for the Gift of Knowledge" (slug gift-of-knowledge). Day 2 now uses Wisdom 7:17 (verified above) instead of Wisdom 9:17, and its "more" gives the seven gifts (CCC 1831) and St. Thomas Aquinas's teaching that the gift of knowledge judges created things rightly, in relation to God, while wisdom concerns God Himself (Summa Theologiae II-II, q. 9, a. 2). The opening prayer and Day 9 prayer now ask for "knowledge and wisdom". The rest of the novena (creation, faith and science, the Logos, knowledge serving love) already fits the gift of knowledge.
