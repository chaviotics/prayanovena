# Novena for Finding Love: Scripture sources

All texts are RSV-CE. Checked word for word on 2026-11-22 against BibleGateway passage pages (`/passage/?search=…&version=RSVCE`, each page header confirmed as "Revised Standard Version Catholic Edition (RSVCE)"). BibleGateway's extracted text prints the divine name as plain "Lord"; "LORD" follows the RSV's small capitals in the Old Testament, as in the other novenas. Proverbs 3:5–6 is the text already verified for `passing-an-exam`.

**No thee/thou.** None of the verses below contains "thee/thou/thy/thine/hast/didst/doth". Tobit 8:5–7 (Tobias's wedding-night prayer, "Blessed art thou…") and Psalm 38:9 were passed over for this reason; Tobit 8:7 alone also starts inside an open quotation. Matthew 5:8 was dropped because the RSVCE opens a quotation mark there.

**Line breaks.** The " / " breaks follow the standard RSV lineation; words and punctuation are verified, the breaks are not.

## Range checks

- No passage starts or ends inside a quotation. John 2:3–5 opens and closes each speech within the range.
- Tobit details used in the reflections, paraphrased and cited by chapter only: Sarah reproached by her father's maids (3:7–9) and praying by her window (3:11); Raphael telling Tobias about Sarah and to pray with her (6:10–17); the proposal communicated to Raguel (7:9); Tobias's call to pray, his prayer "not because of lust, but with sincerity" and to "grow old together", and Sarah's Amen (8:4–8); Raphael's call to praise and thank God (12:6).
- Permission limits: 17 verses in total. No whole book, and far under 25% of any book.
