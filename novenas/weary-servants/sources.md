# Novena for Weary and Wounded Servants: sources

## Scripture (RSV-CE)

All texts are RSV-CE, checked word for word on 2026-11-24 against the RSVCE chapter files of the GitHub repository `andrewheekin/obsidian-holy-bible` (chapter files headed "(RSVCE)", fetched from raw.githubusercontent.com), compared by script after restoring small-caps LORD ("theLord" in the source) and dropping the source's footnote letters in Psalm 23:2–3 ("waters;t", "righteousnessv"). No passage uses thee/thou. Line breaks (" / ") follow the standard RSV lineation; the source shows none, so words and punctuation are verified, the breaks are not.

**Quotation marks.** Turned down because the range would start or end inside a quotation: Matthew 11:28–30 (v.30 closes the speech opened at v.25), John 21:15–17 and 21:17 (v.17 opens "Feed my sheep. and the speech runs to v.19), Isaiah 43:18–19 (v.18 opens God's speech, which runs past v.19), Exodus 18:17–18 (Jethro's speech runs to v.23), Jeremiah 31:25 (ends a long speech), Numbers 11:11–15 (thee/thou). Numbers 11:14 alone has no quote marks. Psalm 42:11 was planned as the epigraph and left for a future spiritual dryness novena (Chav, 2026-11-24).

**Overlap with the companion novena.** Serving in Ministry uses Mark 6:30–31 ("Come away… and rest a while") and Dieric Bouts' Elijah picture for its Day 6, so this novena uses neither.
