# Novena for Weary and Wounded Servants: review

Independent Catholic content review (fresh Opus helper, 2026-11-24), text only. No doctrinal problems found; the saint facts checked out (Carceri, La Verna, Rule for Hermitages, 1220, 1 Celano 103; Toledo 1577, Spiritual Canticle; Darjeeling 10 Sep 1946, "I thirst", posthumous letters).

## Must fix (all applied)
1. Litany title "Mary, Comfort of the Afflicted" → "Mary, Comforter of the Afflicted" (Consolatrix afflictorum), everywhere.
2. Feast lines "Memorial · …" → "Feast · Dec 14" / "Feast · Sep 5" (house style; Teresa is not on the General Roman Calendar).
3. John of the Cross patronage → "patron of Spanish poets".
4. Day 5 paragraph 2: "stripped of his offices" → "left without office"; "a sister" → "a Carmelite prioress" (letter of 6 July 1591 to M. María de la Encarnación).
5. Day 2 paragraph 1: says plainly that Elijah asked to die ("It is enough"), instead of softening it into tiredness.
6. Day 2 step: adds "If, like Elijah, you have wished you could die, tell someone today."
7. Day 1 step: rewritten so it no longer echoes Serving in Ministry's Day 1 step.

## Suggestions applied
- Safeguarding tip adds the civil authorities where a crime may have been committed; Alongside prayer adds "a doctor or counselor".
- Day 5 step allows "If you are not ready yet, ask God for the desire to be."
- Day 6 prayer "who humbly laid down his leadership" (2 Celano 143), not "in peace".
- Day 1 "He invites us, weariness and all"; Day 3 "Habakkuk pictures" (the verse is conditional); Day 1 prayer no longer repeats the opening prayer's "more work" line; Day 6 "God's answer was not 'try harder'" and the tip's "taking a break for a while", to move further from the companion novena's wording.

## Not applied
- Day 4 step pointer "see the tips below": "below" is wrong in some layouts; the tip is on page 1 / How to Pray.

## Picture review (same reviewer, part 2)
Modesty fine everywhere; Days 2, 4, 5, 7, 9 honest to their verses.
- Must fix (applied): Day 3 Luyken *Barren Fig Tree* (Luke 13 is a warning to bear fruit or be cut down, the wrong message for Habakkuk 3:17–18 and for tired servants) replaced; John of the Cross portrait credited to an unknown artist (Commons/Wikidata), not "attributed to Zurbarán"; Day 6 Tissot place left blank (collection not verifiable).
- Suggestions applied: Day 1 alt corrected (who kneels, who sits); Day 4 and Day 6 look lines; Day 6 alt names Jethro; Day 7 place "Royal Collection, on loan to the V&A".
- Kept: Day 8 Moses striking the rock for Isaiah 43:19 (Isaiah reuses the Exodus water image, 43:20; 48:21). The John portrait's identification rests on the museum's label; a better-documented portrait would be an upgrade later.
