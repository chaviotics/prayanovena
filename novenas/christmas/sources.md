## Simbang Gabi (tips)

- 9 Masses Dec 16–24; a Filipino custom from the Spanish era, held before dawn so farmers could attend before work; now at dawn or in the evening (Wikipedia citing Philstar / Filipinas Heritage Library; ADLA guidelines; Abp Villegas, newsinfo.inquirer.net/1875603). Dates such as 1669 and the Sixtus V story are unconfirmed and left out.
- Evening Masses and readings: Archdiocese of Manila 2026 circular, "Weekday evening Masses may use the readings and prayers for the following day, except when Sunday is being anticipated" (interaksyon.philstar.com, 2026-11-17). Hence the tip on matching days.
- Wish belief: Bp Broderick Pabillo, auxiliary bishop of Manila, Philstar 2016-12-19: completing the nine days "is not for their wishes to be granted. That is a wrong belief"; it is preparation for Christmas and thanks to God.

## O Antiphons and the Expectation

- O Antiphons at Evening Prayer, Dec 17–23: Dec 17 O Sapientia (Wisdom), Dec 21 O Oriens (Dawn) (catholicculture.org). Used on Days 2 and 6.
- Expectation of the BVM, Dec 18: fixed by the Tenth Council of Toledo (656); "Nuestra Señora de la O" (Catholic Encyclopedia 1913, "Feast of the Expectation"). HISTORICAL. Called "an ancient Spanish feast", not a current Roman feast. "Our Lady of the Expectation" is used as the litany's Marian title.
