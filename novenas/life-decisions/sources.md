# Novena for Big Life Decisions: sources

## Scripture (RSV-CE)

All texts are RSV-CE, checked word for word on 2026-11-22 on BibleGateway (RSVCE), one passage per page: `https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=<ref>&version=RSVCE`. No passage uses thee/thou.

**LORD.** BibleGateway's page text shows small-caps LORD as "Lord". For Proverbs 16:9 and Jeremiah 29:11 the page's meta description reads "LORD", so "LORD" is used. Luke 1:38 and 1 Corinthians 12:5 have "Lord" (Greek *Kyrios*), not LORD.

**Line breaks.** The fetched text shows no poetry breaks except Proverbs 15:22 (break after "go wrong,"). The " / " in Proverbs 16:9 follows the standard RSV lineation; the words and punctuation are verified, the break is not.

### Range checks and passages turned down

- Every passage starts a sentence and ends in a full stop; the quotations in Isaiah 30:21 and Luke 1:38 open and close inside the passage.
- Turned down: 1 Peter 4:10 (ends on a colon, mid-sentence), Joshua 1:9 (ends with the closing quote of a speech that opens in v.2), Deuteronomy 30:19 ("choose life"; ends on a comma), 2 Timothy 1:7 (starts lowercase "for", mid-sentence). Psalms addressed to God (25:4–5, 143:8, 119:105) have thee/thou.
- Permission limits: 15 verses. No whole book, no book beyond 25%.

### Other Scripture referenced (cited or quoted in a phrase only)

- Isaiah 11:2 "the spirit of counsel and might" (Day 2; verified text already in `novenas/gift-of-knowledge/novena.json`) and Acts 1:14 (Mary prays with the apostles), Day 2.
- John 2:5 "Do whatever he tells you" (Day 9).
