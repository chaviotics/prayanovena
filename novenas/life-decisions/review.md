# Catholic review (2026-11-22)

Independent review by a fresh reviewer with no stake in the draft. No blocking errors. Changes made:

1. Day 1: Newman in 1833 was not "unsure what his life was for" (he wrote "I have a work to do in England"); now "homesick and uncertain of the road ahead".
2. Day 4: "God speaks through our deepest desires" is a later Ignatian reading, not Ignatius's words; the paragraph now says the Exercises begin each prayer by asking for the grace one desires, and "those who follow his way have learned…". Prayer: "who taught us to bring our desires to You".
3. Day 4 reflection: Paul's charisms vs natural talents distinguished ("our natural talents come from Him too").
4. Day 5: peace is "a sign worth heeding, tested over time and with wise counsel" (consolation can be counterfeited, SpEx §332).
5. Day 8: added a word for someone who already chose a path that now feels wrong: nothing is wasted; changing course after prayer and counsel is not failure.
6. Day 7: "Families sacrifice for our schooling" → "Others may have sacrificed for our schooling" (general audience).
7. Tip "Praying for someone else's choice" now rests on CCC 2230 (adult children choose profession and state of life; parents should not pressure).
8. Tip "Alongside prayer": added counselor, priest or spiritual director if worry or indecision becomes too heavy.
9. Day 3: Newman's "I may never know it in this life, but I shall be told it in the next" paraphrased faithfully (no "fully").

Kept: Day 9 step wording (fixed across the library). Checked and fine: CCC 1831, 2116, 2230; Jeremiah 29:11 framed in the exile with no promise of success; Ignatius, Xavier and Newman facts and dates; Suscipe; SpEx §185, 186, 318; titles and names.
