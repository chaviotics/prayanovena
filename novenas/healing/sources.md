# Novena for the Healing of the Sick: Scripture sources

All texts are RSV-CE. Checked word for word on 2026-11-20 with WebFetch.

**No thee/thou (2026-11-21, Chav).** Jeremiah 17:14 (epigraph) and Psalm 23:4 (Day 3) used "thee/thou/thy"; they were replaced with the RSV-CE verses marked in the table, checked word for word on 2026-11-21 (BibleGateway RSVCE and/or EWTN RSV-CE). Reflections, prayers and steps that echoed the old verses were adjusted. Psalm line breaks follow the standard RSV lineation.

**How they were checked.** Passage pages typed from the skill's URL pattern (`/passage/?search=…&version=RSVCE`) were refused because they needed approval, and some passage pages returned the wrong version (LEB or MSG) from a cached session. So each verse was checked on its BibleGateway **verse page** (`/verse/en/…`), which lists the RSVCE text next to other versions. Those pages print the divine name as plain "Lord", so "LORD" capitals were confirmed on EWTN's online Bible, which labels its text RSV-CE. A two-verse passage was checked one verse at a time.

**Line breaks.** Neither source shows poetry line breaks in the fetched text. The " / " breaks in Psalm 147:3 follow the standard RSV lineation; Isaiah 43:2 follows the BibleGateway RSVCE page. The words and punctuation are verified, but the breaks are not.

## Range checks

- No passage starts or ends in the middle of a quotation.
  - Mark 1:40–41 closes both quotations.
  - Sirach 38:13–14 is one full sentence.
  - Mark 2:3–4 ends at the end of a sentence.
- The text quoted in day reflections, prayers and steps comes from these verified passages: "If you will, you can" and "if you will" (Mark 1:40), and "I will be with you" (Isaiah 43:2, Day 3 step). Other Scripture is paraphrased and cited by reference only: John 11:35, Mark 2:5, Tobit 11 and Tobit 12.
- Permission limits: 15 verses in total. No whole book is quoted, and no book is quoted beyond 25%. 3 John has 15 verses (14 in some numberings), and one verse is used.
- The closing verse has 26 words, against the skill's "up to about 25". Kept, because the verse fits the intention exactly: health of body and soul.

## Other Scripture referenced (paraphrased or cited by reference only)

- Tobit 12:15, quoted in St. Raphael's bio: "one of the seven holy angels who present the prayers of the saints".
- John 11:35, Mark 2:5, Tobit 11, Tobit 12, Tobit 12:6, 2 Corinthians 12:8–9.

## Review

An independent Catholic review (review.md) found 3 errors and 8 improvements, and all were applied on 2026-11-20:
- St. Camillus's leg wound: "for the rest of his life", not "all his life".
- At Lourdes, Our Lady told Bernadette to drink and wash at the spring (she didn't tell her to dig).
- Jesus wept before He reached the tomb (John 11:35, 11:38).
- The Anointing of the Sick is explained (CCC 1514–1515).
- Healing that doesn't come is never a sign of weak faith (CCC 1508).
- Added the hope of eternal life (CCC 1505).
- Added a warning against false "sure cures" (CCC 2117).
- Wording fixes on Days 2, 4, 5, 7 and 9.
