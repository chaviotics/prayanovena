# Novena for Growing in Our Talents: sources

## Scripture (RSV-CE)

All texts are RSV-CE, checked word for word on 2026-11-24 against the RSVCE chapter files of the GitHub repository `andrewheekin/obsidian-holy-bible` (commit 9595b0c, chapter files headed "(RSVCE)", fetched from raw.githubusercontent.com; BibleGateway was unreachable from the workspace). No passage uses thee/thou.

**Quotation marks.** Matthew 25:14–15 was turned down: v.14 opens a new paragraph of Jesus' discourse with a double quote that closes only at v.30. Matthew 25:16–18 has no quote marks; 25:21 and 25:24–25 open and close their single-quoted speech inside the passage. Luke 16:10 (dangling opening quote) and 1 Corinthians 12:7 were considered and not used.

**LORD.** 1 Samuel 16:18 has small-caps LORD in the source ("theLord" with markup stripped), so "LORD" is used.

**Line breaks.** The source shows no poetry breaks. The " / " in Psalm 127:2 follows the standard RSV lineation; words and punctuation are verified, the break is not.

## Saints and teaching

|---|---|
| Sloth (acedia) is a capital sin | CCC 1866 (also 2094, 2733) |
| Life and physical health are precious gifts entrusted by God, to be cared for reasonably | CCC 2288 |
| Rejects a cult of the body, idolizing physical perfection and success at sports | CCC 2289 |
| Benedict: studies in Rome, hermit at Subiaco, Monte Cassino | St. Gregory the Great, *Dialogues* II |
| "Idleness is the enemy of the soul"; set hours for manual labor and holy reading | Rule of St. Benedict, ch. 48 |
| Craftsmen to work with humility; a proud craftsman removed from his craft; "that in all things God may be glorified" (1 Pet 4:11) | Rule, ch. 57 |
| Abbot arranges things so the strong have something to strive for and the weak are not driven away (paraphrase) | Rule, ch. 64 |
| "a little rule for beginners" | Rule, ch. 73 |
| Benedict patron of Europe | Paul VI, *Pacis nuntius*, 1964 |
