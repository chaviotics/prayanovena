# Novena for Growing in Our Talents: Catholic review

Independent review on 2026-11-24 by a fresh reviewer with no stake in the draft (doctrine, saints' facts, no guarantees, sacraments, kindness, titles). Confirmed correct: all Scripture against the RSV, CCC 2288 and 2289, Benedict's dates, feast, patronage and Rule chapters 48, 57, 64 and 73, Bosco's dream at nine, dates and feast, Paul's references. Nothing read as superstition, guarantees, prosperity teaching or self-help.

## Must fix (applied)
- Day 5 reflection: CCC 1866 only lists sloth/acedia; the definition is now tied to CCC 2094 (weariness toward spiritual good, refusing the joy that comes from God), and the cure is "small faithfulness, sustained by grace".
- Day 6 story: in the *Memoirs of the Oratory* Bosco prayed the Rosary with the children and repeated the sermon *before* his tricks, not after.

## Suggestions (applied)
- Day 6: names the chastity context of 1 Corinthians 6:19–20 before widening it to all of bodily life.
- Day 4: names the ordination context of 2 Timothy 1:6–7 ("That gift was unique, but the lesson reaches every gift of God").
- Day 1 story: "each one is a real trust from God" (the old line contradicted "faithful over a little").
- Day 9: the master "gave to each according to his ability" (the old wording overreached the parable).
- Day 2 story: "a Pharisee blameless under the Law (Philippians 3:5–6)" replaces an anachronism.
- Bosco bio: "reason, religion and loving kindness", the standard wording of his triad.
- Day 7 prayer names St. Paul; Day 7 reflection ends "Fame fades, but love remains."
- Day 8: rest no longer framed as a productivity tool.
- Day 9 prayer: less repetition of "for Your glory and the good of others".
- Alongside prayer tip: Morning Offering by name, "or a priest", and a line for people limited by illness, disability or age and for the scrupulous ("A missed day is not a sin").

## Noted, not changed
- The parable's judgment ending (Matthew 25:26–30) is left out, as fits a devotional; Day 3 reads the servant's fear as a false picture of God, in line with recent papal preaching.

## After the review (Chav, 2026-11-24)
- Each day names only the saint whose story fits; paragraph 2 carries the saint moment; prayers keep a light touch.
- New Day 3 "Seeing the Gifts in Us" (1 Samuel 16:18; Paul and Timothy, Acts 16:1–2, 2 Timothy 1:6) for people who cannot yet see their own talents; the old Day 4 "Rekindle the Gift" was folded into "Faithful in Little Things". Day 1 and the "Praying for someone else’s gifts" tip mention discovering gifts. Not re-reviewed by the independent reviewer.
