# Catholic review (independent reviewer, 2026-11-22)

Verdict: sound, kind and doctrinally careful; no promise of a child anywhere. CCC 2374, 2379, 1654 and 1261 quoted or paraphrased correctly; Day 6 faithful to Donum Vitae II.B.5; Protoevangelium details correct and always framed as "an ancient tradition"; Our Lady of Hope is an approved title (Pontmain 1871; "Mother of Hope" added to the Litany of Loreto in 2020).

Applied:
- MUST: Day 9 prayer "Your love and Your timing" → "Your love and Your wisdom" ("timing" quietly promises a child later).
- Day 6 more: IVF teaching stated as teaching ("not morally acceptable", CCC 2376–2377), cites Dignitas Personae 13, and adds "His mercy is open to every couple" for those who already used IVF.
- Opening prayer: St. Joachim "who knew the sorrow of waiting" (was "shame", prayed daily).
- Day 2 refl: "Luke's Gospel sets that idea aside" (was "Scripture", since parts of the OT link fertility with blessing).
- Zechariah silent "until John was named" (Luke 1:59–64), bio and Day 5; Day 5 "God did not take back His gift because of his doubt" (was "patient with his doubt").
- Day 9 more: Luke 1:38 paraphrased (rule: Scripture outside the day's verse is paraphrased).
- Tip "If the road is hard": names the blessing for parents after a miscarriage or stillbirth and funeral rites for the baby.
- Day 7: Isaiah 25:8 replaced by Psalm 34:18 ("the reproach of his people he will take away" could read as a promise that childlessness ends). Revelation 21:4 was considered and turned down (dangling closing quotation mark).
- Day 1 more: "Anne comes from the Hebrew name Hannah"; "her husband's other wife".
- Day 6 prayer: "every choice that is right in Your sight" (was "in good conscience").
- Day 8 more: uniting the cross with Christ's (CCC 2379).
- Tip "Alongside prayer": names restorative reproductive medicine.
- Kicker: "9 days of hope in the waiting" (the reviewer's "9 days of hope for couples longing for a child" repeated the title on the link preview).

Not applied: naming the Protoevangelium of James in the app text (kept in sources.md to save words).
