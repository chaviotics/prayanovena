## Patterns

Recurring solutions, one line each: the pattern, then the products that use it.

- Daily reminders for the 9 days: Intercede, Pray More Novenas (email), Catholic Novenas: Daily Prayer.
- Praying a novena together as a group: Intercede.
- No imprimatur or named priest review shown on the prayers: every product above.
